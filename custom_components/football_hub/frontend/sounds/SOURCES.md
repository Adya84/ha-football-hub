# Football Hub alert sound sources

The referee whistle, ding and boo recordings are from WilhelmSFX, released under CC0 1.0:
https://github.com/Wh1teDuke/WilhelmSFX

The referee recording is used as recognisable football match signals: one blast for kick-off, two for half-time and three for full-time.

The stadium goal cheer is `Crowd Cheering in Stadium` by vishiv, distributed under the Pixabay Content License and obtained from the attributed web-ready copy in:
https://github.com/opstiger/aiba-percent-battle/blob/main/assets/aiba-audio/SOURCE.md
